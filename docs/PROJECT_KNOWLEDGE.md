## 3. Core Business Services & Pillars

### Service Pillar 1: HVAC Works (Comfort | Efficiency | Reliability)
End-to-end commercial heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning solutions engineered for energy efficiency, indoor air quality, and consistent thermal control:
- **VRF / VRV Systems**: Multi-zone variable refrigerant flow systems for offices and commercial spaces.
- **Ductable & Package AC Systems**: Centralized cooling solutions for retail showrooms, banquet halls, and open workspaces.
- **Chillers & AHU Installation**: Large-scale chilled water systems and Air Handling Units.
- **Cassette & Hi-Wall AC Units**: Clean, discreet ceiling cassette and wall units.
- **HVAC Ducting, Grilles & Dampers**: Galvanized iron (GI) ductwork, motorized dampers, diffusers, and acoustic lining.
- **Piping & Insulation**: High-grade copper refrigerant piping, nitrile rubber insulation, and gravity condensate drainage.
- **Testing, Commissioning & AMC**: Air balancing, CFM verification, refrigerant leak testing, and proactive AMC.

### Service Pillar 2: MEP Works (Integrated Systems | Safe | Efficient)
Comprehensive mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering:
- **Electrical Solutions**: LT/HT electrical distribution, main LT panels, distribution boards (PDB/LDB), cable trays, structured wiring, architectural and functional lighting, earthing, lightning protection, and power load management.
- **Plumbing & Drainage**: Potable water supply networks, sanitary drainage, booster pumps, submersible pump sets, rainwater harvesting connections, and STP/ETP integration piping.
- **Fire-Fighting Systems**: Fire hydrant systems, automated sprinkler networks, diesel and electrical fire booster pumps, fire alarm panels, smoke detectors, and emergency manual call points.
- **Associated Infrastructure**: Building Management System (BMS) integration, utility coordination, and unified MEP containment.

### Service Pillar 3: Interior Fit-Out Works (Functional | Aesthetic | Turnkey Solutions)
Commercial interior turnkey execution blending architectural appeal with functional utility:
- **Ceilings & Partitions**: Gypsum board false ceilings, modular grid ceilings, acoustic baffle ceilings, and glass/drywall partition systems.
- **Flooring & Finishes**: Vitrified tiling, carpet tiles, epoxy coatings, and premium architectural painting.
- **Carpentry & Joinery**: Custom reception counters, workstations, storage units, executive cabinetry, and wall paneling.
- **Retail Showrooms & Store Fit-Outs**: Visual merchandising fixtures, illuminated display gondolas, and shopfront glazing.
- **Office & Commercial Interior Execution**: Complete turnkey delivery matching architectural drawings.

### Service Pillar 4: Operation & Maintenance (AMC Services)
Keeping commercial spaces operating without interruption:
- **Preventive Maintenance Schedules**: Scheduled seasonal audits, filter cleanings, coil chemical cleaning, and electrical tightening.
- **System Health Checks**: Thermal imaging, vibration analysis, and current draw benchmarking.
- **Rapid Response & Emergency Support**: On-call technicians to eliminate operational downtime.
- **Extended Asset Life**: Preserving manufacturer warranties and maximizing system lifespan.

## 4. Execution Methodology & Process

### Our 5-Step Working Approach:
1. **Understand Requirements**: In-depth site survey, architectural drawing review, and clear client objectives definition.
2. **Plan & Coordinate**: Detailed MEP 3D coordination, heat-load calculations, load balancing, and material submittals.
3. **Execute with Precision**: Professional site supervision, milestone-driven installation, and adherence to safety protocols.
4. **Test & Commission**: System pressure testing, airflow CFM measurement, electrical megger testing, and operational stress runs.
5. **Support & Maintain**: System handover with documentation, client team training, and scheduled AMC support.

### Quality, Safety & Execution Framework:
- **Site Coordination**: Seamless alignment between architects, general contractors, structural engineers, and facility teams.
- **Installation Checks**: Strict punch list inspections against approved shop drawings and national building codes.
- **Testing**: Pre-commissioning verification of all electrical, hydraulic, and air circuits.
- **Commissioning**: Final tuning and environmental optimization.
- **Handover Documentation**: Complete as-built drawings, test certificates, operation & maintenance manuals, and warranty documents.
