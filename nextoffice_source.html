How We Work

Our 6-Step Design to Build Process

01

Understanding Your Requirements

Every office project starts with a conversation. We look at your space, team size, budget and what you need from the workplace before moving into the office design and planning.

02

Early Design Ideas

Once we understand your requirements, we put together initial office design ideas so you can see how the space could look and work before we move ahead.

03

Clear Cost Estimate

Once the design is taking shape, we work out the expected office interior cost based on your space, requirements and budget. We also help you choose sustainable options that fit your project and budget.

04

Final Design Review

We make the final changes to the 3D office design and share the complete view with you, so you know exactly what the space will look like before work starts.

05

Smooth Project Execution

Once the design is approved, we move to the site and get the work underway. Our team coordinates the contractors, materials and day-to-day work to keep your office interior and fit-out moving as planned.

06

On-Time Project Delivery

We set a clear timeline at the start and stay on top of the work throughout the office fit-out, so you know where the project stands and when your office will be ready.

Who is
About Us

NextOffice brings workplace planning, office interiors and fit-out together under one team. From the first idea to the final handover, we keep the process clear, practical and well managed. 

Why Choose Us

Experience You Can Rely On

Sustainable Design

We consider sustainability from the start of every project. This includes the materials, finishes and vendors we choose, helping us create sustainable office interiors that are comfortable, practical and made to last.

Post-Occupancy Support

Even after your office is ready, we're here when things change. If your team grows or you need to change the layout, NextOffice can help with office reconfiguration, workspace changes and fit-out updates.

Design Backed by Research

We look at how your teams actually use the office before deciding on the workplace design. This helps us plan practical layouts that make it easier for people to focus, meet, collaborate and get their work done.
